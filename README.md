# Exclusive TV

A premium IPTV player for Android TV, designed for a smooth and simple viewing experience.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Live TV & EPG**: Watch your favorite channels with a full TV guide.
- **AI Voice Search**: Find channels and content just by speaking.
- **Fast Loading**: Optimized for quick stream starts and smooth playback.
- **Favorites**: Save your most-watched channels for quick access.
- **Multi-Device**: Use your subscription on multiple TV devices.
- **Auto-Updates**: Always stay up-to-date with the latest features.

## 🚀 Get Started

1. Download the latest version from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/CodesRahul96/Exclusive-TV-Updates/releases).
2. Install the APK on your Android TV or FireTV stick.
3. Login with your phone number and enjoy!
